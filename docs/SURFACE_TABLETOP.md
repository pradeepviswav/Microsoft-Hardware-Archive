# Surface Tabletop / PixelSense

Added two previously omitted records from original Microsoft content. IDs 162 and 163 preserve all existing saved-device identities. Broad category: Displays. Product line: Surface Tabletop / PixelSense, distinct from Surface RT/tablets, Studio and Hub.

- Surface 1.0: 30-inch tabletop announced in May 2007; commercial AT&T rollout followed in April 2008. The record uses announcement year and explains the rollout date.
- Samsung SUR40: direct successor using Surface 2.0 software and PixelSense sensing, unveiled in January 2011 and shipping by January 2012. The record uses shipping year and credits Samsung manufacturing. Specs include the 40-inch Full HD screen, AMD processor/graphics, 4-inch thickness, mounting options, and contact-point count.
