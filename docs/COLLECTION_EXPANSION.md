# Collection expansion · 9 November 2026

Expanded the archive from 25 to **161 records**, adding **136** independently identified models and product lines. The earliest entry is the **1980 Z-80 SoftCard**. The complete collection covers **20 product families**.

## Sources and scope

Discovery began with the [Microsoft hardware index on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_hardware) and [Microsoft Store accessories](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/b/accessories). Each addition has recorded specifications, a research date, and one or more source links visible in its detail dialog. **101 additions use Microsoft documentation or an archived Microsoft announcement as their primary source**; the remainder link to Wikipedia histories and model articles. Microsoft sources were preferred for specifications when a model-specific page remained accessible.

Important archive sources include:

- [Microsoft Hardware Product Catalogue 2013](https://download.microsoft.com/documents/uk/hardware/Consumer_HW_Brochure_FY13_FINAL.pdf): original specifications and individually identified photographs for older peripherals. Product photographs were extracted directly from the PDF, including their embedded transparency masks.
- [Microsoft Accessories Press Materials](https://news.microsoft.com/accessories-press-materials/): dated historical fact-sheet index for mobile keyboards, mouse models, and wireless display adapters.
- [Microsoft Hardware's 2007 history](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2007/08/22/a-microsoft-milestone-hardware-celebrates-25-years-of-proven-success/), contemporary input-device announcements, and the Windows hardware blog archives.
- [Surface device lifecycle reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/surface-driver-firmware-lifecycle-support) and [Surface accessory lifecycle reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/surface-accessories-driver-firmware-lifecycle-support): date cross-checks, rather than a promise of current support or availability.
- Individual Microsoft Support model specifications and feature pages, Microsoft Learn developer documentation, and Microsoft Store product details.

This is a curated static collection, not an exhaustive inventory or a live stock feed. The Store selection records products listed on the provided accessories page on 9 November 2026; stock and regional availability can change. It excludes colour editions and keyboard/pen bundles already represented by their component products. Known product generations remain separate. The second-generation hard-drive Zune and flash Zune are distinct designs; storage capacities within the flash line are grouped.

Dates represent introduction or release years, with context where announcements and availability differ. Surface Laptop SE is recorded by its 2022 release. Surface Keyboard (2nd Edition) has **no asserted introduction year** because the reviewed official product page did not establish it; the UI labels its year as unconfirmed and sorts it after dated records. Kin is in **Phones**, since it used Windows CE rather than Windows Phone. Nokia models retain Nokia in their names. Existing record IDs 1–25 are preserved for saved-device compatibility.

## Images

**114 additions have a locally stored photograph or manufacturer diagram**, including **21 support diagrams**. Together with the original 25 images, this gives **139 illustrated records**. The other **22** additions are explicitly marked “Photo not yet catalogued.” A wrong model, a Microsoft logo, and a blank retired Store asset are not used as substitutes. Shared manufacturer diagrams are labelled and explained when the original support page reuses an illustration across models.

Photographs and diagrams link to their original source. Wikimedia images include credited authors and available license links. Files are resized and converted to WebP. Full image provenance is recorded in [IMAGE_SOURCES.md](../IMAGE_SOURCES.md), and the record/source index is in [data/catalogue-provenance.json](../data/catalogue-provenance.json).

## Browsing and verification

The collection loads 24 records at a time, while search, family filters, era filters, sorting, saves, and comparison operate on the complete catalogue. Filtering resets the visible page size. “Microsoft Store selection” narrows to the distinct accessories in the supplied Store listing; it does not claim they are in stock.

Automated checks validate stable IDs, unique names, source coverage, model years, null-year handling, local image files, progressive loading, Store selection, photo placeholders, filters, persistence, keyboard dialogs, comparison limits, and mobile layout. Browser checks load every available local product image. Desktop/mobile screenshots are refreshed in this pull request.
