## Targeted historical corrections

- Xbox 360 did not introduce Xbox Live. [Microsoft's original Xbox Live launch announcement](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2002/11/15/xbox-live-arrives-in-stores-sparking-the-next-revolution-in-video-games/) dates the North American service launch to November 2002.
- The flattening mouse description belongs to the 2010 Arc Touch model, rather than the folding 2008 Arc Mouse. [Microsoft's September 2010 announcement](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2010/09/01/think-the-mouse-is-dead-think-again-with-microsofts-arc-touch-mouse/) describes this mechanism. Its replacement photo depicts the Arc Touch model.
- The original keyboard photograph represented the Natural Keyboard Elite, a successor to the original 1994 Natural Keyboard. The entry now identifies the Elite rather than pairing a 1994 name/date with its later image. [Model history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Natural_Keyboard) and [replacement Elite image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MS_Natural_Keyboard_Elite.png).
- Xbox Series X description now qualifies 4K / 120 FPS as supported-game capabilities rather than promising both universally. It avoids an evergreen "most powerful to date" claim.

Historical entries without a primary announcement link remain explicitly labeled as not independently reverified in this refresh.
