# Shared report reconciliation · 10 November 2026

Input: [Technical Analysis and Archival Documentation of the Microsoft Hardware Ecosystem](https://gemini.google.com/share/9b326aaa6e72).

The report is a discovery aid, not an authoritative specification sheet. The collection now distinguishes broad categories from named product lines. Surface Pro, Laptop, Laptop Go, Laptop Studio, Book, Studio, Duo, and Hub are separate lines; IntelliMouse, Natural, Sculpt, SideWinder, Lumia, Kin, Zune, and adaptive input lines are likewise grouped explicitly. Individual model records and stable IDs remain intact.

Corrections to avoid propagating from the report:

- Surface Studio graphics differ by generation; RTX graphics must not be assigned to the original Studio or Studio 2.
- Surface Duo and Duo 2 have different panel sizes. Keep their individual recorded specifications.
- NVMe storage and hardware ray tracing describe Xbox Series hardware, not all Xbox One models.
- Adjustable-tension thumbsticks belong to Elite Series 2; do not generalize them to the first Elite controller.
- Samsung SUR40 is a later partner implementation of PixelSense, not a renamed original Microsoft Surface tabletop.
- Production windows ending in “Present” are not reliable evidence of current manufacturing, stock, or support.

Further research leads include Surface Hub 3, Microsoft Audio Dock, Display Dock HD-500, Surface Precision Mouse, early expansion cards, RoundTable, and SPOT partner watches. These require individual records and primary-source checks rather than automatic import of the report's technical claims.

Azure Kinect DK specifications were expanded with its 1 MP depth sensor, 12 MP rolling-shutter colour sensor, 440 g weight, and dimensions, checked against [Microsoft’s hardware specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/azure/kinect-dk/hardware-specification).
